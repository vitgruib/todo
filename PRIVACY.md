# Toto Simple Privacy Policy

## Data handling

Toto Simple does not require a Toto Simple account and does not collect, sell, or share your task
data with the developer. The extension contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

By default, tasks, archived tasks, and settings are stored only in the extension's local browser
storage on your device. Toto Simple does not make network requests to a Toto Simple server.

## Optional Sync Across Devices

Sync Across Devices is off by default. If you choose to enable it, task data is stored and
transferred using your browser's sync service for the signed-in browser account you select. This
allows the same tasks to appear on your other signed-in browsers.

Toto Simple does not operate its own task server or receive a copy of synced task data. Your
browser provider's privacy policy and sync settings govern data handled by its sync service.

## Deleting data

Turning off Sync Across Devices removes the tasks Toto Simple saved to the browser sync store.
Removing the extension removes its local browser storage. If you use browser-provided sync, check
your browser's sync settings for any additional data-management options.
